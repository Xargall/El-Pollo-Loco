Class: ThrowableObject

ThrowableObject(x, y)

Represents a thrown salsa bottle in the game world. Flies in an arc, rotates during flight, and plays a splash animation on impact.

Constructor

new ThrowableObject(x, y)

Creates a new ThrowableObject at the given position. Loads all animation frames, generates a unique ID, and starts the throw and animation loops.
