Class: Chicken

Chicken()

Represents a regular Chicken enemy in the game. Moves erratically once awake and takes multiple hits to defeat.

Constructor

new Chicken()

Creates a new Chicken instance. Loads walking and dead animations, sets a random horizontal starting position, a random speed, and a unique ID.
